Governed parameters are numbers and rules that the company supplies (productivity factors, hire terms, calendar, escalation, payment terms, benchmark ranges, take-off factors ...). The software ships none. Each is validated, needs a stated source, goes Draft → Pending Review → Approved by a permitted human, and an approved value is never edited — a change is a new version. Only Approved values affect the strict estimate.

Propose a parameter

Quick starts: work calendar · payment terms · plant-hire terms · escalation · productivity factor · time allocation. Every field is blank: fill each from your own records and name the source.
