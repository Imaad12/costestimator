Rates workbook

Bring prices in from Excel in four steps. Nothing becomes a rate until a different person approves it: uploaded rows are candidates that wait in the Approvals inbox. Original files are stored untouched with their SHA-256 hash.

1Download the template
Yellow cells are inputs. The full price list already lists every material, labour, plant, equipment and subcontract item in the catalogue - you only type the prices you have.

Download full price list (.xlsx) Blank template

Already have a price list in your own layout? Skip this: upload it below and tell the system which column is which.
2Fill it in
One price per row: resource id, unit, unit price, currency (required, never assumed), price date, validity, tax basis, delivery basis and an evidence reference.
3Upload and check
You see accepted and rejected rows with the reason for each before anything is stored.
4Import, then approve
Candidates are reviewed, published as Pending Review rates, and approved by a different person.
